## What's new

- **Questions only for your licence.** A profile with no licence chosen was getting questions from every licence in topic practice, topic tests and the quick exam, so tractor, bulldozer and hydraulics questions showed up next to car ones. Home now asks you to pick your licence first if none is set, and Home's licence picker no longer has a "not selected" option.

## Installing

Install `Prava-EN.apk` over the app you have. Your progress stays. From v1.7.0 on, Home shows a card when a newer version is out.
