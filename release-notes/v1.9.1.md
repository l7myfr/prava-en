## What's new

In **Rules → 12 Priority and Intersections**, two tram rules that decide common exam cards:

- **Two trams follow the car rules between themselves**, including the left-turn rule: a tram turning left gives way to an oncoming tram going straight or right. Example: card #50, where the order is tram A → tram B → yellow car → red car.
- **Trams lose their priority on an extra-section green arrow.** At traffic lights a tram normally goes first, but a tram going on the green arrow while its main light is red or yellow must give way to everyone from other directions. Example: card #107, where the tram driver gives way.

Both are in English and Georgian.
