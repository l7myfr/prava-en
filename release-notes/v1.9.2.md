## What's new

In **Rules → 10 Traffic Lights and Officer Signals**:

- **Picture guide to the special traffic lights**:
  - reversible-lane lights (green arrow down, red X, yellow diagonal arrow, switched off)
  - a green arrow in an extra section next to a red main light
  - an extra section that is switched off
  - arrow-shaped lights
  - the T-shaped bus/tram light
  - the flashing reds at railway crossings
- **Two "switched off" rules the exam asks about**:
  - A reversible-lane light that is **off** means you may **not** enter that lane (card #142).
  - An extra section that is **off** closes its direction. The outline arrows on the green show what's still allowed (cards #148, #149).
- **A green left arrow also lets you U-turn** (card #157).

Everything is in English and Georgian.
