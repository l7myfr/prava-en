## What's new

In **Rules → 10 Traffic Lights and Officer Signals**:

- **Every light at a glance.** One table with every light the law describes, what each one means and the exam cards that ask about it: the normal colours (red + yellow means stop, green is coming next: card #161), flashing yellow, arrows, extra sections, outline arrows, all four reversible-lane signals, the T-shaped light, pedestrian and bicycle lights, the railway-crossing lights, and lights versus priority signs.
- **Trams and traffic lights.** When a tram goes first, and the one case where it gives way.
- **The special-lights picture guide** now also shows the pedestrian light, the bicycle light, the T-light's lamp for each direction, and all three railway-crossing states: flashing red = stop, flashing yellow = go with special caution (card #155), switched off = go only if no train is in sight (card #180).

Checked against article 29 of the Road Traffic Law. Everything is in English and Georgian.
