## What's new

In **Rules → 18 Emergencies, Accidents and First Aid**:

- **Skeleton diagram.** A labelled skeleton shows where each bone is, with the exam rule for each one. Green marks the one-bone parts where a tourniquet goes (upper arm, thigh), red the two-bone parts where it doesn't work (forearm, shin), plus the pulse point on the neck.
- **Bandages section.** A picture sheet of the bandage types (circular, spiral, figure-of-eight, spica, sling-type, pressure bandage, arm sling, tourniquet) and what each one is for, the first-aid kit, which bandage to use for which injury, how to bandage, and the exam traps. For example: the ankle always gets a **figure-of-eight**; spica and sling-type are the wrong answers.
- **Burns corrected.** Sterile pad, but no bandage roll. Then ice, a painkiller and water, as the exam answer says.

Everything is in both English and Georgian.
